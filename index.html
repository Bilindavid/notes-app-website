Zen Notes

A fast, private, offline Notes app. Capture your thoughts, pin what matters, and find anything in a tap — all stored securely on your own device.

About

Zen Notes is a simple and elegant note-taking app built with React Native. It works entirely offline — your notes never leave your phone. There are no accounts to create and no sign-in required. Just open the app and start writing.

Available for Android. Supports light & dark themes and multiple languages.
